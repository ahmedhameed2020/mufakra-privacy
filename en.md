
# Privacy Policy — MUFAKRA

**Last updated:** 1 October 2026
**Developer:** Ahmed Abdelhamid
**Contact:** privacy@mufakra.app

## In short

MUFAKRA is an offline-first app. Your students, lessons and payments are **stored on your device only**. We run no server, we do not collect your data, and the app has no ads, trackers or analytics.

## What the app stores

You enter it yourself, and it stays in a database on your device:

- Students: names, phone numbers, parent phone numbers, subjects, addresses, notes.
- Lessons, groups, schedules, homework and attendance.
- Payments, opening balances and write-offs.
- App settings and preferences.

**We never receive any of it.** This is personal data about your students and their families; you are responsible for handling it lawfully where you live.

## Permissions and why

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications and exact alarms | Lesson and payment reminders, scheduled on your device. |
| Contacts (optional) | Importing a student from your contacts, only when you ask. Never read in the background. |
| Biometrics / screen lock (optional) | Locking the app with your fingerprint or face. Handled entirely by the device OS. |
| Files | Saving a backup or PDF report, or importing a student list from Excel, where you choose. |
| Internet | Only to open WhatsApp or links, and for Google Drive sync if you turn it on. |
| Run at startup | Rescheduling reminders after the device restarts. |

## WhatsApp and links

Tapping "Send via WhatsApp" opens WhatsApp with a prepared message; you press send. The message and number are handed to WhatsApp on your device and are covered by its privacy policy. The app never sends messages by itself.

## Backups and Google Drive sync (optional)

- **Manual backup:** a file you save yourself, optionally protected with a password (AES-GCM). We do not know your password and cannot recover it.
- **Automatic sync:** if you sign in with Google, an encrypted copy of your data is uploaded to **the app's hidden folder in your own Google Drive** (`appDataFolder`).
  - The only scope requested is `drive.appdata`: the app can reach its own folder and cannot see your other Drive files.
  - The files live in your account; the developer cannot access them.
  - They are subject to Google's privacy policy and terms.
  - You can turn sync off and sign out at any time, and delete the copy in Drive's "Manage apps".
- The sign-in token is kept in the device's secure storage (Keystore/Keychain).

MUFAKRA's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell or share your data.
- No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting that sends data out.
- We do not use your data to train AI models.
- No account is needed to use the app.

## Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can erase everything from Settings, or uninstall the app to remove it from the device. Copies you uploaded to Drive or saved as files remain until you delete them.

## Children

Students in the app may be minors, but the user is the adult teacher, who enters their data and is responsible for any consent needed from parents or guardians. The app is not intended to be used by children themselves.
